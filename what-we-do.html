What We Do

We keep a record of the things a small business owner needs to know but is easy to forget, and we tell you when you need to know them.

License and certification dates.

Renewals, expirations and continuing education due dates for you and your people.

Insurance and paperwork renewals.

Policy renewals, bonds, registrations and other dates that carry a penalty if missed.

Worker overtime.

Hours and overtime that are easy to lose track of. You tell us the numbers and the limit that matters to you.

Anything else you carry in your head.

If it is a date or a number you need reminding about, we can hold it.

Reminders at the cadence you choose.

Text, email, or both. Weekly, daily, or only when something is due.

Your information, on request.

Ask for everything we have, or a single item, whenever you want it.

What we do not do

  • We only know what you tell us. We cannot promise you will never miss a date.
  • We do not give legal, tax, insurance or payroll advice.
  • We do not renew, file or pay anything for you.
  • We do not ask for passwords or access to your other systems.

Fit

Built first for small contractors and trade businesses, where licenses, certifications and overtime pile up. Other small businesses with the same problem are welcome to ask.

Pricing is set after a short conversation about what you need us to keep.
