Owners carry a lot of small facts: when a license renews, whose certification is about to lapse, who is close to overtime. Some are in email, some in payroll, some on paper, and some nowhere. We store them and remind you at the right time.

You send it.

Email us a list, forward a message, or send a photo. Whatever form it is in.

We keep it.

We write down what you sent and send it back to you to confirm. Nothing counts until you say it is right.

We tell you.

A short text, email, or both, when something is coming up. You choose how often and when.

How It Works

  • Nothing to log in to.

    There is no app, dashboard or account to keep up. Your information is always available to you on request.

  • No spam.

    You are not bombarded. You pick the cadence, and we only write when there is something to know.

  • No passwords.

    We do not ask for logins to your payroll, email or other systems. You tell us what is in them.
