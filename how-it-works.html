How It Works

Do you guarantee approval?

No. The platform and your customer decide. We make sure nothing we control is the reason it stalls.

Do you log in to my account?

Only a named person you authorize in writing, with their own username and password. We never ask for yours.

Who writes my safety programs?

A qualified safety professional, quoted separately. If you already have programs, we review what you have first.

What does it cost?

Pricing is set after a short conversation about your accounts.

The ISNetworld or Avetta fee is paid by you directly to them.

What do you need from me?

A user for our named person, your hiring-client list, insurance certificates, EMR letter, OSHA logs and hours or incident data, current written programs, licenses, and training records.

How fast does it start?

Your first status report arrives about a week after we receive your documents and your authorization.
