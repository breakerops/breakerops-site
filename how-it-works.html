How It Works

How do I give you my information?

Email it to us. A list, a forwarded message, a photo of a card or a note all work. You do not need a special format.

How do I know you got it right?

We write back what we recorded, with the date and what it is for. You confirm or correct it. Items we are unsure about are marked unknown, not guessed.

How do you tell me when something is coming up?

By text, email, or both, whichever you prefer. You pick how often and how far ahead. Reply to tell us something is done or to stop. Replies are read and handled by us, not by an instant automatic system, so allow some time.

Do I have to log in to anything?

No. There is no dashboard to keep up. Ask us for what we have and we send it.

Do you need access to my payroll or other systems?

No. We do not ask for passwords. You tell us what is in them and we keep the record.

Do you guarantee I will never miss a date?

No. We can only remind you of what you have told us. Keep us up to date and we will do our part.

What does it cost?

Pricing is set after a short conversation about what you need us to keep.
