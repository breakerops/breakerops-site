SMS Terms

Last updated October 18, 2026

BreakerOps works over text message. These terms cover the texts we send you.

What you are signing up for

When you enter your mobile number and click "Get started", you agree to receive text messages from BreakerOps about setting up your account and, once set up, your morning day text and the reminders you ask for.

How often we text

Message frequency varies with your use: setup messages when you join, one morning text each day your account is active, and a text for each reminder you set.

Rates

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message to stop all texts. You will get one final text confirming you are unsubscribed. Reply HELP for help, or email matt@breakerops.com.

Delivery

Texts are sent through mobile carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages, and delivery is not guaranteed.
