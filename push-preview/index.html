Add BreakerOps to your Home Screen

  1. In Safari, tap the menu beside breakerops.com in the address bar, then Share . If you already see the Share symbol in your toolbar, tap it directly.
  2. Scroll down the Share menu and tap Add to Home Screen. If missing, look under Edit Actions.
  3. If shown, leave Open as Web App on, then tap Add.
  4. Open the new BreakerOps icon from your Home Screen. Sign in there, then tap Enable notifications.

This button shows the steps. iPhone requires the actual Home Screen action in the browser menu. If you are inside another app's browser, open this page in Safari first.
