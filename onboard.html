Get Started

Tell us what you want us to keep track of. Check the boxes that apply, add what you know, and add anything of your own. It takes about five minutes. There is no login.

How this works: fill in what you know and skip the rest. When you press Send, it goes to our inbox. We write back what we recorded and you confirm it. Nothing is tracked and no alerts go out until you confirm.

Do not send passwords, Social Security numbers or bank details. We do not need them.

About you

How you want to hear from us

Texting customers is not switched on yet. For now alerts come by email, and we will tell you when text is ready.

What to keep track of

Tax filing/payment dates

Federal, state, sales tax, quarterly estimates, extensions. Date and what it is for.

Employee birthdays (month/day only)

Name and month and day only. Please do not send birth years.

OT warning thresholds (workweek start + hours source)

Which day your workweek starts, the weekly hours that should trigger a warning, and where we will get the hours (you tell us, or a weekly list you send).

Business/trade license expirations

Contractor, electrical, plumbing or other trade licenses. License name and expiration date.

Worker certification/training expirations

Who, which certification or training, expiration date. OSHA cards, CE hours, safety training.

Insurance renewals

General liability, workers' comp, auto, bonds, certificates of insurance. Policy and renewal date.

Vehicle registration/inspection

Truck name or plate, what is due, due date.

Equipment inspections/service

Lifts, meters, calibration, ladders, tool testing. Item and next due date.

Permit renewals/deadlines

Permit, jurisdiction, renewal or inspection deadline.

Payroll submission/pay dates

Payroll cutoff days, pay days, payroll tax deposit dates.

Anything else

Add your own. Give each one a name, then what and when.
