Privacy

Last updated October 15, 2026

BreakerOps keeps records and sends reminders for small business owners. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it. Your information stays yours. We never sell it.

What we collect

Your name, email and business name; the dates, deadlines and notes you give us by text or email; and, if you connect an account, the calendar, email or workspace data that account lets us read.

Connected accounts

You choose what to connect. Connecting Google, Microsoft or Slack lets us read the items we need to find dates and deadlines and set reminders. You can disconnect at any time, and you can also revoke access from your Google, Microsoft or Slack account settings. Disconnecting stops us from reading new items from that account.

How we use it

Only to run your calendar and reminders and to support you. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. We do not train our own AI models on your connected-account data.

BreakerOps use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see it

Our team, and the service providers we use to store data and send email and text messages, only as needed to run the service. Those providers handle data under their own terms, which we do not control. We share information if the law requires it.

Passwords and sensitive data

Please do not send passwords, Social Security numbers or bank details by text or email. We will never ask you to.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you are a customer. Disconnecting an account does not by itself erase information we already saved. You can ask us to delete your data at matt@breakerops.com. We will review each request and tell you what we removed and anything we still need to keep.
